## 🚀 Currently working on

### ⚡ [quintrit](https://github.com/velurex-labs/quintrit)
**High-performance, memory-efficient inference for 1.58-bit ternary LLMs with 5 trits/byte encoding and division-free SIMD kernels.**

`1.58-bit LLMs` `SIMD Kernels` `Ternary Arithmetic` `Inference Optimization`

> 💡 I’ve always been fascinated by optimization algorithms and bit-level tricks, and by how much can be gained by rethinking the arithmetic itself. That curiosity led me to explore efficient ternary multiplication, and quintrit grew out of it. It sits well outside my day-to-day .NET work and comfort zone, which is exactly why I enjoy it.

## 🧠 Recently learned

Recently, my study and hobby projects have all revolved around AI, developing across three distinct dimensions:

1. **Implementing AI & Agent Workflows** — Integrating generative AI into real-world applications and mastering the surrounding ecosystem:  
   `RAG` `MCP` `Embeddings` `Vector DB` `Tool Calling` `ReAct` `Prompt Engineering` `Fine-tuning`

3. **LLM Internals & Architecture** — Looking under the hood to understand the inner workings, layer structures, and low-level arithmetic of modern language models — the very insights that enabled me to design and build [quintrit](https://github.com/velurex-labs/quintrit).
